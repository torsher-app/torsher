# Support Torsher

Torsher is open source under the AGPL-3.0 license and built by one developer. Donations are its only funding: there are no paid features, no ads and no telemetry.

## Donate

Donation links appear with the first public release.

| Platform | For | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| PayPal | One-time or monthly, by card or from a PayPal account | <Badge type="info" text="soon" /> |
| Open Collective | One-time or monthly, by card, with public accounting | <Badge type="info" text="soon" /> |

## What donations pay for

- **Development time:** the server, the TV showcase, the control panel and this documentation.
- **Test hardware:** TVs, an Android TV box and NAS models that Torsher has to work on before it claims support for them.
- **Infrastructure for the project itself:** the domain and this site.

## What donations don't buy

- **Features.** Every feature is in the open-source release for everyone. Donors get nothing extra in the app.
- **Content.** Torsher does not include, host or index any content, and donations have nothing to do with the sources you connect.
- **Priority support.** Bug reports are handled the same way for everyone.

## Other ways to help

- **Report bugs** in [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/torsher-app/torsher/issues): what you did, what you expected, and the log from <Ui k="panel.nav.logs" /> in the panel.
- **Report a release title Torsher reads wrong:** the title, and what season, episode, quality or voice-over it should have found.
- **Test on your hardware:** a NAS, an older LG TV or an ARM board, and tell how it went.
- **Star the repository** on [GitHub](https://github.com/torsher-app/torsher) and tell people who might like it.

Pull requests to the main repository are not accepted; issues and bug reports are welcome.
